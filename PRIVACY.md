# Privacy Policy

The MIXI privacy policy is published with the documentation site, so there is a
single canonical version instead of two copies that can drift apart:

### → **https://www.mixidaw.com/privacy**

Source file: [`docs/privacy.md`](docs/privacy.md)

It covers:

- **The documentation site** — hosting, no cookies, no analytics, no third-party
  trackers, and the GDPR data-controller details.
- **The MIXI application** — what is stored locally (IndexedDB / localStorage
  keys and retention), what is *not* collected, audio recordings, the
  watermarking tiers, third-party services, children's privacy and your rights.

Please make edits in `docs/privacy.md` so the published page stays authoritative.
